Our Story

Brew Haven Coffee Shop started in 1989 as a small roadside coffee shop. We served fresh coffee with care and friendly service. With the love and support of our customers, we grew step by step. Today, we are proud to have 43 branches across India. We continue to serve quality coffee, tasty food, and friendly service. Our goal is to make every customer feel happy and welcome. Thank you for being a part of our journey.
